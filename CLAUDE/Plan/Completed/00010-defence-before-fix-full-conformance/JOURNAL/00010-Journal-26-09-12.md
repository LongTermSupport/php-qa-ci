## 09:03 · decision · T2.3, T3.3 — the Owner said resolve them; here is how

The Owner's instruction was "resolve these blockers and wrap this up". Decision 3 reserved
both of these to the Owner; that instruction is the Owner exercising it by delegation, so the
decisions are taken here, marked as taken on that instruction, and are reversible by
reopening the task. Recorded as Decisions 5 and 6 in `PLAN.md`.

**PHPArkitect (2.3): accepted gap, shipped default unchanged.** The two "conforming"
options were both worse than the gap. Parsing arkitect's prose into per-rule identifiers is a
second implementation of an output format we do not own — silently broken by every upstream
wording change, and a false "identified" is worse than an honest "not identified". Dropping
the bundled tier removes structural defences from every consumer to improve a scorecard,
which the plan's Non-Goals forbid by name.

**PHPStan native catalogue (3.3): not carried offline, and `rule-doc` now says so
usefully.** `bin/rule-doc method.notFound` answered `Unknown rule identifier` — true and
useless to someone holding the string. It now says the identifier is not php-qa-ci's, names
the phpstan.org page, and states that the catalogue is not carried offline with a pointer to
the declaration. That is not conformance and is not claimed as such. Vendoring the catalogue
was costed: hundreds of pages, staleness shorter than PHPStan's release cadence, no defect
behind it.

## 09:04 · decision · — — Decision 7: two link checkers stay; `forbiddenAttribute` stays

The `markdownLinks` / hooks-daemon `pointer-resolves` overlap the Owner spotted: kept
deliberately. One is a guardrail for the editor seconds after the edit, on this checkout only;
the other is the package's guarantee to every consumer on every run. `docs/tools/markdownLinks.md`
now says so, and also no longer claims to be "last in the linting phase", which it had not
been since `yamlLint` and `shellCheck` were added.

`phpqaci.forbiddenAttribute`: not renamed. It is published, and a consumer may carry it in
`ignoreErrors`; a rename is a breaking change with no defect behind it.
